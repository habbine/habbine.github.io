Jitka Čábelická v. Czech Cycling Federation & Czech Olympic Committee

Habbine Estelle KimJul 27, 2024

On 26 July 2024, the ad hoc Division of the Court of Arbitration for Sport (TAS/CAS) issued its decision in the case of Jitka Čábelická (the “Applicant”) v. the Czech Cycling Federation (CCF) and the Czech Olympic Committee (COC) (the “Respondents”) (CAS OG 24/08).

The Applicant had challenged the decisions of the CCF and the COC concerning the selection of athletes for the “Women’s Elite” category of the cross-country mountain bike event at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games. She had asked the CAS ad hoc Division to declare that the CCF and the COC had failed to comply with the applicable nomination criteria and to order a reconsideration of the nomination for the Paris 2024 Olympic Games, so as to secure the Applicant’s participation.

The Panel, chaired by Ms Carine Dupeyron (FRA) and composed of arbitrators Prof. Roberto Moreno (PAR) and Dr Heiner Kahlert (GER), dismissed the appeal a few hours before the opening ceremony of the 2024 Olympic Games.
