August 2024 – Court of Arbitration for Sport (TAS/CAS) – Ad Hoc Division

Habbine Estelle KimAug 5, 2024

On 3 August, the Court of Arbitration for Sport (TAS/CAS) registered the following cases:

Adrien Coulibaly v French National Olympic and Sports Committee (CNOSF) (CAS OG 24/11)

Mr Adrien-Mansa Coulibaly is a runner in the French delegation. He filed an application with the CAS Ad Hoc Division challenging the decision of the French National Olympic and Sports Committee (Comité national olympique et sportif français, CNOSF) not to enter him in the 4x400 m relay at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games (the Challenged Decision). The Applicant asks the CAS Ad Hoc Division to set aside the Challenged Decision and to authorise the entry of an additional athlete for the 4x400 m relay.

Ms Carine Dupeyron (FRA) sat as Sole Arbitrator at the hearing held on 3 August 2024.

David Sánchez López v Turkish Weightlifting Federation (CAS OG 24/12)

David Sánchez López is a Spanish weightlifter. He filed an application with the CAS Ad Hoc Division challenging the decision of the Independent Member Federations Sanctioning Panel (IMFSP) of the International Weightlifting Federation dated 22 July 2024. That decision concerns a financial sanction imposed on the Turkish Weightlifting Federation (TWF) following three positive doping cases involving three of its athletes.

Dany Brand v World Athletics (CAS OG 24/13)

Dany Brand is a Swiss athlete. He filed an application with the CAS Ad Hoc Division asking it to rule on his eligibility to compete in the men’s 400 m hurdles at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games, following the withdrawal of Ludvy Vaillant (FRA), who had received a quota place in the reallocation phase and was not replaced by another French athlete after his subsequent withdrawal through injury. He contends that he should now receive the quota place as the next eligible athlete.

Mr Lars Hilliger (DEN) sat as Sole Arbitrator at the hearing held on 3 August 2024.

Decision of 2 August 2024

Tatiana Debien v United World Wrestling (UWW) & International Olympic Committee (IOC) (CAS OG 24/10)

Ms Tatiana Debien is a French wrestler. She filed an application with the CAS Ad Hoc Division challenging the decision of the International Olympic Committee (IOC) of 27 July 2024 (the Challenged Decision). That decision rejected her request for an additional quota place for France in women’s 53 kg wrestling. The application failed for lack of jurisdiction of the CAS Ad Hoc Division, since the dispute arose no later than 10 July 2024, when UWW published the allocation of quota places.

The Panel, chaired by Mr Philippe Sands KC (GBR/FRA/MRI), with Ms Raphaëlle Favre Schnyder (SUI) and Mr Hamid Gharavi (IRI/FRA) as arbitrators, declared that it lacked jurisdiction to rule on the case.
