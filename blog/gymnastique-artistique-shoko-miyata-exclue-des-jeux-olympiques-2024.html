Artistic Gymnastics – Shoko Miyata Excluded from the 2024 Olympic Games

Habbine Estelle KimJul 22, 2024

Shoko Miyata, aged 19, is captain of the Japanese women’s artistic gymnastics team.

According to Kenji Nishimura, Secretary General of the Japan Gymnastics Association, the gymnast was seen smoking in a private setting in Tokyo and drinking in a room at Japan’s National Training Centre in late June/early July 2024.

The day after an investigation was opened on 15 July 2024, Shoko Miyata admitted to the Japan Gymnastics Association (JGA) that she had been drinking and smoking cigarettes.

The JGA Code of Ethics

The Code of Ethics of the Japan Gymnastics Association (JGA) provides that athletes must refrain from any inappropriate conduct, including consuming alcohol outside regulated limits and using illicit substances.

“where athletes, coaches and judges significantly fail to comply with the obligations of athletes, coaches and judges as stipulated in the competition rules and scoring rules; where a person violates the rules of the Association; (...) if the person commits any other violation”

(Source: https://www.jpn-gym.or.jp/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/jgar…)

Japanese law

Since the ninth year of the Meiji era (1876), the age of majority in Japan had been set at 20.

On 1 April 2022, the amended Japanese Civil Code came into effect, lowering the age of majority to 18.

However, this change does not affect the legal age for alcohol and tobacco. Smoking and drinking therefore remain prohibited in Japan for anyone under 20.

JOC regulations

Under the regulations of the Japanese Olympic Committee (JOC), athletes must observe strict protocols governing their public conduct, particularly during competition periods:

“Policy: athletes and officials of the Japanese national team must be worthy of representing the people of Japan, observe courtesy and discipline, and serve as role models for society. Code of conduct: comply with laws and regulations and do not engage in illegal activities or make statements that would damage the honour and trust of athletes and officials of the Japanese national team. (...) 8. As a rule, smoking is prohibited, even for persons aged 20 or over, at venues where the Japanese national team is participating. A total ban will be implemented gradually over several years from 2016. Drinking alcohol is prohibited even if the player is aged 20 or over.” (unofficial translation)

(Source: https://www.jpn-gym.or.jp/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/JGA_…)

Sanctions

The breach of the rules of conduct led to the Japanese gymnast’s immediate exclusion from the Paris 2024 Olympic Games, where she had been due to compete at Bercy Arena on 28 July 2024.

The gymnast was accordingly sent back to Japan from the team’s training base in Monaco.
