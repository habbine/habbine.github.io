Athletics – Florentina Costina Iusco Suspended for Doping on the Eve of the Paris 2024 Games

Habbine Estelle KimJul 26, 2024

On 25 July 2024, the Court of Arbitration for Sport (TAS/CAS) partially upheld the appeal filed by the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) against the decision issued by the hearing panel of the Romanian National Anti-Doping Organisation on 1 February 2024 (the Challenged Decision). In the Challenged Decision, Romanian long jumper Florentina Costina Iusco had been found to have committed an anti-doping rule violation, but had been sanctioned only with a reprimand and no period of ineligibility, on the basis of No Significant Fault or Negligence.

On 23 April 2023, Florentina Iusco underwent an out-of-competition doping control. Analysis of her A sample revealed the presence of furosemide, a diuretic listed under category “S5 Diuretics and Masking Agents” of the WADA 2023 Prohibited List. Furosemide is classified as a “specified substance” prohibited at all times. Analysis of the B sample confirmed the presence of the substance, corroborating the A-sample results.

In its appeal before CAS, WADA sought the imposition of a two (2) year period of ineligibility starting from the date on which the CAS award takes effect.

The Sole Arbitrator appointed to hear the case found that Florentina Iusco had not demonstrated that she met the standard of care required to establish “No Significant Fault or Negligence”. He therefore decided to impose a two (2) year period of ineligibility, starting on 1 February 2024, the date on which the Challenged Decision was issued.

The Challenged Decision was accordingly amended as follows:

  • Florentina Iusco is suspended for a period of two years starting on 1 February 2024.
  • All competitive results obtained by Florentina Costina Iusco between 23 April 2023 and 24 July 2024 are disqualified, with the forfeiture of any medals, points and prizes.
