CAS Appeal Dismissed – Beijing 2022 Olympic Winter Games – Team Figure Skating

Habbine Estelle KimJul 26, 2024

On 25 July 2024, the Appeals Division of the Court of Arbitration for Sport (TAS/CAS) issued its decision dismissing the appeals filed by the Russian Olympic Committee (ROC) (CAS 2024/A/10355), the Figure Skating Federation of Russia (CAS 2024/A/10360), and the Russian skaters Aleksandr Galliamov, Nikita Katsalapov, Mark Kondratiuk, Anastasia Mishina, Victoria Sinitsina and Kamila Valieva (CAS 2024/A/10356) (the Appellants) against the re-ranking decision of the International Skating Union (ISU) of 30 January 2024 (the Contested Decision). The Contested Decision concerned the results of the figure skating team event at the Beijing 2022 Olympic Winter Games (OWG).

The Appellants had asked the CAS to re-rank the figure skating team event and to award the gold medal to the ROC. Following the hearing held on 12 June 2024, it was decided that Ms Valieva’s results had been correctly disqualified in the Contested Decision, and that the ROC skating team could not be awarded the gold medal.

On 22 July 2024, a hearing was held in a related but separately filed appeal (CAS 2024/A/10354) brought by the Canadian skaters Madeline Schizas, Piper Gilles, Paul Poirier, Kirsten Moore-Towers, Michael Marinaro, Eric Radford, Vanessa James and Roman Sadovsky, together with Skate Canada and the Canadian Olympic Committee (COC) (the Canadian Appellants).

The Canadian Appellants also asked the CAS to re-rank the figure skating team event, with the following results: United States (gold); Japan (silver); Canada (bronze).
