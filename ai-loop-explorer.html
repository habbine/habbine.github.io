Sources and how to read the evidence tags

Sources were searched on 7 Nov 2026. The map states no measurements, only mechanisms and dated hypotheses, so it stays valid when numbers change. Re-check each hypothesis at the source named in its panel. Primary means the original document from an agency, regulator, central bank, statute or research firm, which I opened. Secondary means a figure relayed by press or trade press. Institution means a report by an international or research institution, not peer-reviewed. Estimate means a modelled projection by a research firm or bank. Peer-reviewed means a published academic paper. My framework means analysis, not a sourced fact. Leverage labels, what-if scenarios and rule links are my judgement. This tool groups related relationships into 30 links. Data providers, developer tools and hardware lifecycle are not drawn.
